🛑 Runaway at load

The original script is just while (true) {}. JavaScriptCore terminates it after 50 ms, and the static HTML around the <script> stays as the fallback. In twidget the same loop is interrupted after 500 ms of continuous execution, the runtime is stopped for good and whatever was rendered before the loop stays on screen, frozen. The loop itself is left out of this port (the corpus rules forbid a tight loop).
